# Disclaimer

ProofStamp via Arbitrum is experimental open-source software. The current application uses **Arbitrum Sepolia testnet** and is not a production service.

## What a ProofStamp can show

A ProofStamp can provide evidence that a particular SHA-256 file fingerprint was recorded in a public blockchain attestation at a particular blockchain time.

## What it does not prove

A ProofStamp does not, by itself, prove:

- who created or owned the file,
- when the underlying content was originally created,
- whether the content is true, authentic, accurate, lawful or unaltered before stamping,
- the identity or authority of the person controlling the attester address,
- that a file is legally notarized, legally signed, admissible evidence, or sufficient for any regulatory requirement.

The proof concerns specific file bytes and the public record of their fingerprint.

## Testnet limitations

Arbitrum Sepolia is a test network. Testnet infrastructure, accounts, attestations, RPC endpoints, sponsorship and explorer services may change, reset, fail or become unavailable. Testnet records should not be relied on for production, legal, financial, compliance or archival purposes.
